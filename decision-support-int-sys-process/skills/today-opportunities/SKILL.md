# Today Opportunities

Read `skills/analysis-doc-standards.md` first, then the Loop Earplugs Today Opportunities document in `~/work/analysis/reports/`. Write `{Brand} — Today Opportunities.md` in `docs/analysis/<slug>/`.

This document is today's fix, not a second copy of the Site Opportunity Report. Write it after that report, or after the same bundle work. Use the full bundle. Recompute the latest week (Monday–Sunday, through the window's last day) before choosing the three actions. A leak that closed last week is not today's plan.

## What qualifies as an action

Three switches, about an hour in total, each in effect the day it is made. A switch is a setting: pause or re-point an ad set, change a destination URL, cap a geo, add a UTM template. It is not a rebuild, a flow, or a test that takes weeks to read.

Pick the three leaks that are still open in the latest week and that stop when the setting changes. Paid-media waste comes first because tomorrow's spend is committed tonight. If the site has fewer than three live settings leaks, write the ones that qualify and say the rest are queued. Do not invent a third.

Each action names an owner, a time estimate, the exact campaigns or URLs (ids as they appear in the bundle), what to do, what not to do (pause versus re-point), and the expected result as an upper bound.

## Document order

1. Header. Title pattern: `Today's Plan: <the leak in plain language>`. One paragraph: the three switches, that the latest week still shows them, and that no new creative or code is required. A callout on why these three and not the larger ideas.
2. Contents.
3. **01 The Situation This Morning.** Latest-week orders and the steady channel, then a three-row table: leak, latest reading, what it is worth. One chart of the leaking channel's revenue per visit by week against the site.
4. **02–04.** One action each, in descending size.
5. **05 How to Read Tomorrow.** A scoreboard: metric, current value, target. Direction in a few days; size after a week on a small site, three or four days on a large one. Say how much one order moves the rate. Note that a promotion landing inside the reading window moves orders on its own.
6. **06 The Rest of the Week.** The larger plays from the Site Opportunity Report, in a when / action / size table. Deliberately not today's work.
7. **A Data and Method.**

Do not put cart recovery, a homepage redesign, or a threshold test in actions 02–04. Those belong in section 06.
